# Carry-On

A music player for iPhone that works without signal. Add your MP3s, then play them anywhere, including in Airplane Mode. It has shuffle, repeat (all songs or one song) and playlists.

## 1. Put it online (once)

Carry-On has to be opened from a web address once, so it can save itself to your phone. GitHub Pages is free:

1. Create a public repository on GitHub, for example `carry-on`.
2. Upload all nine files in this folder (on a phone: open `github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/carry-on/upload/main` in Safari, tap **choose your files**, tap **Select**, pick them all, then **Commit changes**).
3. In the repository, open **Settings → Pages**. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, pick `main` and `/ (root)`, then **Save**.
4. After a minute or two it's live at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/carry-on/`.

Any host that serves files over HTTPS works too.

## 2. Install it on your iPhone

1. Open that address in Safari.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open Carry-On from your Home Screen. The label at the top right changes to **READY OFFLINE** once it has saved itself.
4. Tap **Add songs** and pick MP3s from the Files app.

Add songs from the Home Screen app, not from Safari. On iPhone the two keep separate storage, so songs added in Safari won't show up in the installed app.

## 3. Before you fly

- When you import, make sure the MP3s are downloaded to your phone and not only stored in iCloud.
- Turn on Airplane Mode, open Carry-On, lock the screen and check a song keeps playing.

## Good to know

- Songs are copied into the app on your phone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- Deleting a song in Carry-On doesn't touch the original file in Files.
- Removing Carry-On from your Home Screen can delete its songs and playlists, so keep the original files.
- Tap the big time on the display to switch between time played and time left.
- Drag the display's bar to skip around in a song.
- If you change the files on GitHub later, the app picks up the new version the second time you open it with signal.
